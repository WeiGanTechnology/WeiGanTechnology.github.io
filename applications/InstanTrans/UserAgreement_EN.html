1. The Service

1.1 InstanTrans is a real-time captioning tool for iPhone. It can:

  • transcribe speech from the microphone you select, or playback audio from other apps after you start a system broadcast;
  • translate captions into the target language you choose, using system translation, and show them in the app or in a Picture in Picture window;
  • keep a limited caption history on the device, which you may export as a subtitle file;
  • after you turn it on, sync caption preferences and history to your own iCloud;
  • check the App Store for a newer version.

Features depend on the installed version, the system version, and the device. Whether a language supports on-device speech, system speech, or system translation is decided by the operating system. We may update, change, or remove features from time to time.

1.4 Recognition and translation run on your device by default. We do not operate a server that receives your recordings or captions. Only in the limited cases described in the Privacy Statement may system speech recognition be processed by Apple. The file for the local enhanced model is downloaded from a model host the first time it is needed; recognition with that model still runs on the device.

1.5 Screen audio starts only after you open the system broadcast and confirm it. The capture handles playback audio from other apps and does not record the screen image. Some apps, protected content, or calls may block system audio capture, in which case captions may be empty or may stop. The broadcast picker does not include a microphone. To caption what you say, switch the in-app audio source to the microphone.
