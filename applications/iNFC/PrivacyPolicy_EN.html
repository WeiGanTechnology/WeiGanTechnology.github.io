Privacy Statement for iNFC

Last updated:2026.7.14

iNFC is an application provided by Weigan Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "we" or "us") for NFC read/write and related device connectivity features. This Privacy Statement applies to iNFC on iOS and Android.

We attach great importance to the protection of your personal information and privacy. We will provide appropriate security measures for your personal information in accordance with legal requirements and industry-standard security practices.

Depending on how you obtain the app, which operating system you use, and your device language environment, advertising and billing features may differ: the Google Play Android build always uses Google AdMob and Google Play Billing, regardless of device system language; on the iOS App Store build, when your device system preferred language is not Simplified Chinese, Google AdMob and Apple In-App Purchase are used, and when your device system preferred language is Simplified Chinese, Pangle GroMore mediation (including Pangle and Tencent Ads) is used and VIP is still offered through Apple In-App Purchase; the mainland China app-store Android build also uses GroMore mediation and does not include Google Play Billing.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Information

We will only use your personal information where we have a lawful basis to do so. Under applicable law, we may process your information based on lawful grounds such as your consent, where necessary to perform or enter into a contract with you, or where necessary to comply with legal obligations.

1.1 Core app features. To provide NFC read/write, Bluetooth device connection, firmware updates, QR code scanning, and LAN-based device updates, we invoke NFC, Bluetooth, camera, and network capabilities through system APIs only when you use the related features. NFC tag data, device communication payloads, and files you choose to read or write are processed on your device or between your device and the peripherals you connect. We do not use this data to build a profile that identifies you.

1.2 Local preferences. We store certain settings on your device (such as theme, usage quota counters, and VIP status flags) to operate the app. This information remains on your device unless otherwise described in this Statement.

1.3 What we do not do. We do not require you to create an account to use iNFC. We do not provide, include, or distribute copyrighted game files or other third-party content through the app.

1.4 Personal information inventory. To meet applicable privacy laws and app-store compliance requirements, we disclose below the categories of personal information that iNFC may involve. The purpose, method, and scope described for each category apply only to the versions or scenarios noted. Categories marked as "not collected" mean that neither our app nor the third-party SDKs we have configured to restrict collection will actively collect, read, or upload that type of information.

  • Android ID: Not collected on the mainland China app-store Android build (disabled in ad SDK privacy settings). On the Google Play Android build, the Google AdMob SDK may automatically collect Android ID when serving ads — purpose ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; method automatic SDK collection; scope device identifiers necessary for advertising services.
  • ICCID, IMSI, MEID, phone number, hardware serial number: Not collected.
  • IMEI: Not collected on the mainland China app-store Android build (disabled in ad SDK privacy settings). Not actively collected by our app on other builds.
  • MAC address: Not collected on the mainland China app-store Android build (disabled in ad SDK privacy settings). Bluetooth features are used only to connect peripherals you choose; MAC addresses are not uploaded to our servers.
  • OAID (Open Anonymous Device Identifier): Collected only on the mainland China Android build after you agree to this Privacy Statement, by Pangle / Tencent Ads SDKs — purpose ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; method automatic SDK collection; scope the anonymous device identifier assigned for advertising services.
  • Sensor list and specific sensor types (such as accelerometer, gyroscope, and other publicly available sensor types on the device): Not collected on the mainland China app-store Android build (disabled via Pangle SDK motion_info and Tencent Ads SDK sensor_ts settings). Core NFC and Bluetooth features do not depend on reading the sensor list. On the Google Play Android build, Google AdMob may perform similar collection when serving ads; see Section 2.1.
  • Location information: Not collected for advertising on the mainland China Android build (disabled in ad SDK settings; coarse-location permission declarations for ads removed from the manifest). On Android 11 and below, the OS may require location permission for Bluetooth scanning; this is unrelated to ad targeting and location data is not uploaded to our servers. On iOS, location may be requested only when you actively use map or tag-coordinate features.
  • Wi‑Fi list, SSID, BSSID, Wi‑Fi state: Not collected on the mainland China Android build (Wi‑Fi state reads for advertising disabled). We do not collect Wi‑Fi lists, SSID, or BSSID.
  • Installed application list: Not collected on the mainland China Android build (disabled in ad SDK settings; query all packages permission removed from the manifest).
  • IP address: On the Google Play Android build, Google AdMob may automatically collect IP addresses when serving ads — purpose ad loading, anti-fraud, and SDK stability; method automatic SDK collection; scope network addresses related to ad services. On the mainland China Android build, after you agree and ad SDKs are initialized, Pangle / Tencent Ads SDKs may obtain IP addresses during ad requests and measurement — purpose ad loading, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; method automatic collection during network communication; scope network addresses related to ad services.
  • Clipboard: We write content to the system clipboard only when you actively trigger a copy action (for example, copying a device identifier). We do not read or upload clipboard content in the background.
  • Images: Accessed only when you actively choose an image from your library to decode a QR code, or save a QR code to your library. Images are processed locally and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Camera: Used only when you actively use QR code scanning. Images are decoded locally and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Video and audio: Rewarded and other ad formats may load and play media through third-party ad SDKs when you watch ads. We do not collect or upload your personal video or audio files, and we do not access the microphone.
  • Calendar, SMS, contacts, system account information, call logs: Not collected.

Collection by third-party advertising SDKs listed above occurs only after you tap Agree on the first-launch privacy dialog (mainland China Android build) or when you use the relevant ad services (other builds). If you choose Decline and use core features only, we do not initialize advertising SDKs and the related collection does not occur.

2. Third-Party SDKs and Advertising

2.1 Google AdMob advertising (iOS: system preferred language is not Simplified Chinese; Android: Google Play build)

This section does not apply to the mainland China app-store Android build. That build does not use Google AdMob, and we do not collect Android ID through AdMob on that build. See Section 2.2 below.

In these scenarios, the app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display ads (including app-open, banner, and rewarded formats), measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. AdMob is provided by Google LLC and may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device information (such as device model and operating system version; platform-specific identifiers such as Android ID on Android or IDFV on iOS): Purpose — ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — parameters necessary for advertising services.
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID on Android; advertising-related identifiers on iOS where permitted by the system): Purpose — ad measurement and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — device-assigned advertising-related identifiers. You can limit ad tracking or reset advertising identifiers in your device settings.
  • Network and diagnostic information (such as IP address, network type, and crash or diagnostic signals related to ad serving): Purpose — ad loading, fraud prevention, and SDK stability; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — data related to ad delivery and SDK operation.
  • Sensor list and specific sensor types (such as accelerometer and gyroscope): Purpose — ad anti-fraud, performance optimization, and device compatibility checks; Method — automatic collection by the SDK through system APIs; Scope — sensor types and list information directly related to advertising and anti-fraud.

Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland), the Google Play Android build may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before serving personalized ads. You can review or change your ad privacy choices on that build in the app under Settings → Ad privacy settings when available for your region.

On iOS in the AdMob scenario described above, the app does not use UMP and does not currently present an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before showing AdMob ads. You can limit personalized ads or reset advertising identifiers in iOS system settings (for example, Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

VIP subscribers do not see ads for features covered by their active subscription.

2.2 Pangle GroMore mediation (iOS: system preferred language is Simplified Chinese; Android: mainland China app-store build)

In these scenarios, the app does not use AdMob. It initializes third-party advertising SDKs through Pangle GroMore mediation, which may serve ads from the following ad networks we have integrated:

  • Pangle (Chuanshanjia) — Beijing Juliang Engine Network Technology Co., Ltd.
  • Tencent Ads (Youlianghui / GDT) — Shenzhen Tencent Computer Systems Co., Ltd.

GroMore selects and fills ad requests across these networks for the ad placements you trigger in the app. After you agree to this Privacy Statement, the SDKs automatically collect information such as:

  • Device information (such as device model and operating system version; IDFV on iOS, and IDFA on iOS where you authorize ATT): Purpose — ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — parameters necessary for advertising services.
  • OAID (mainland China Android build only; Open Anonymous Device Identifier): Purpose — ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — the anonymous device identifier assigned for advertising services.
  • Network state information (such as network type and connectivity): Purpose — ad loading and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — data related to network connectivity.
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic information (such as impressions, clicks, conversions, and SDK operational logs): Purpose — ad measurement, settlement, and troubleshooting; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — data directly related to ad services.
  • IP address: Purpose — ad loading, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection during network communication by the SDK; Scope — network addresses related to ad services.

SDK providers and privacy policies:

  • Pangle / GroMore: https://www.csjplatform.com/privacy/partner
  • Tencent Ads (GDT): https://e.qq.com/dev/help_detail.html?cid=2005&pid=5983

Pangle and Tencent Ads each maintain independent privacy disclosures for their SDKs. Beyond the common categories listed above, whether an SDK processes additional personal information, and details such as retention periods or cross-border transfers, are governed by the relevant provider's privacy policy.

Privacy controls configured in our app (by platform):

Mainland China Android build. To follow a data-minimization approach, we configure Pangle SDK privacy settings and GroMore mediation privacy settings to disable or restrict collection and use of the following. We do not initialize advertising SDKs until you tap Agree on the first-launch privacy dialog:

  • Android ID
  • Device MAC address
  • Installed application list (we do not supply a custom app list to the SDK; the app manifest also removes the query all packages permission required by the ad SDK)
  • IMEI and similar telephony identifiers
  • Location information (for advertising) (the app manifest removes the ad SDK's coarse location permission declaration; location permission used for Bluetooth scanning is required by the operating system for device discovery and is unrelated to ad targeting)

  • Sensor list and specific sensor types (disabled via Pangle SDK motion_info and Tencent Ads SDK sensor_ts)

iOS (system preferred language is Simplified Chinese). We configure the Pangle SDK privacy provider to disable use of location for advertising. Before initializing the Pangle SDK, if the system has not yet recorded your choice, the app may present an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt to determine whether IDFA may be used. If you deny or restrict tracking, we configure the SDK to limit IDFA use. Unlike the mainland China Android build, iOS does not currently show the same first-launch Agree/Decline privacy dialog; VIP subscribers do not see ads. iOS does not involve Android-specific identifiers such as Android ID, OAID, or IMEI.

Third-party SDKs may transmit the above information to their servers for processing and storage. Please refer to each provider's privacy policy for details.

4. Device Permission Usage

This service may request the following permissions on iOS and/or Android. We ask for your consent through the system permission dialog before using sensitive capabilities. Permissions you deny will limit only the related features.

NFC

Used to read from and write to NFC tags and cards when you use NFC features (iOS and Android).

Bluetooth (scan and connect)

Used to discover and connect to external Bluetooth devices (such as Pixl.js) for data transfer and firmware updates (iOS and Android). On Android 12 and above, Bluetooth scan is declared as not used for location purposes.

Location

Android: On Android 11 and below, the operating system may require location permission before Bluetooth device scanning. This permission is not requested on Android 12 and above for Bluetooth scanning in this app. It is unrelated to ad targeting on the mainland China build, where coarse location permission declarations for the ad SDK have been removed from the manifest.
iOS: Location permission may be requested when you use map or tag-coordinate related features. Such data is used only for features you actively invoke and is not used directly for ad targeting.

Photo library (iOS)

Used when you actively choose an image from your library to decode a QR code, or when you save a QR code to your library from a related feature. Access occurs only when you trigger the related feature; we do not read your entire photo library in the background.

Face ID / Touch ID (iOS)

Used for local authentication when you enable app lock or related security features. Biometric data is processed by the system; we do not collect or store your biometric templates.

Camera

Used for QR code scanning when you choose that feature. Camera images are processed locally for decoding and are not uploaded to our servers (iOS and Android).

Internet and local network

Used for ads, store billing, firmware or content downloads you initiate, and LAN-based device updates (iOS and Android).

Notifications (Android)

Used to show optional status notifications related to background device operations (such as Bluetooth connections or updates), when you grant notification permission.

Foreground service — connected device (Android)

Used to keep Bluetooth or device-connection tasks running reliably while you are actively using related features.

You can manage permissions for this app at any time in your device Settings.
