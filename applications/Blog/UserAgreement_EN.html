  • Home feed: browse latest, featured, and followed blog posts, plus news and knowledge base articles;

  • Classrooms: view assignments, announcements, and polls; submit assignments, vote, and comment; users with management permissions may also publish assignments, announcements, and polls and manage class members;
  • Search and discovery: search posts, users, and related content;
  • Account hub: view profile, bookmarks, your blog, and settings;
