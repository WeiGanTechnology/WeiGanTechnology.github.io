Privacy Policy for RetroSpider

Last updated:2026.8.30 · 简体中文

RetroSpider is a classic Spider Solitaire casual puzzle game provided by Weigan Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "we" or "us"). This Privacy Policy applies to RetroSpider on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, visionOS, Android, and HarmonyOS.

We attach great importance to the protection of your personal information and privacy. We will provide appropriate security measures for your personal information in accordance with legal requirements and industry-standard security practices.

This Privacy Policy works together with our User Agreement for RetroSpider.

Match progress, save data, statistics, and settings in RetroSpider are handled primarily on your device. We do not upload your match library to our servers. The Service currently does not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, and we do not use ads to unlock core gameplay. Optional RetroSpider Pro subscriptions or lifetime purchases are offered through each platform's app store to unlock cosmetic features such as themes, table backgrounds, and card backs.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Information

We will only use your personal information where we have a lawful basis to do so. Under applicable law, we may process your information based on lawful grounds such as your consent, where necessary to perform or enter into a contract with you, or where necessary to comply with legal obligations.

1.1 Core app features. To provide Spider Solitaire gameplay, undo/redo, hints, auto-complete, daily challenges, save management, statistics, and achievements, we process match state, save data, statistics, and challenge progress locally on your device. This data is used to restore matches and display game records. We do not use it to identify you personally, and we do not upload your full match library to our servers.

1.2 Local preferences. We store certain settings on your device (such as themes, table backgrounds, card backs, sound, animation speed, hint strategy, Pro entitlement flags, and UI preferences) to operate the app. This information remains on your device by default.

1.3 In-app purchases and entitlement verification. If you purchase a RetroSpider Pro monthly subscription, annual subscription, or lifetime unlock, purchase confirmation, subscription status, and product identifiers are returned by Apple, Google, Huawei, or other app stores to unlock the corresponding benefits on your device. We do not collect or store your full payment card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials.

1.4 Diagnostics and troubleshooting (where applicable). On some desktop builds, the app may generate local diagnostic logs or crash summaries for troubleshooting. You may choose to export such files. This information is not uploaded to our servers by default.

1.5 Custom themes (where applicable). On supported platforms, you may import or export theme files. Files are chosen by you and stored locally or in folders you specify. We do not actively read or upload your other personal files.

1.6 What we do not do. We do not require you to create an in-app account operated by us. We currently do not integrate third-party advertising or user-behavior analytics SDKs such as Firebase, Umeng, Pangle, or AdMob. We do not read your photo library, contacts, SMS, call logs, or phone number in the background, and we do not request camera, microphone, location, Bluetooth, or other sensitive permissions unrelated to core gameplay (subject to your actual build).

1.7 Personal information inventory. To meet applicable privacy laws and app-store compliance requirements, we disclose below the categories of personal information that RetroSpider may involve. The purpose, method, and scope described for each category apply only to the versions or scenarios noted. Categories marked as "not collected" mean that our app does not actively collect, read, or upload that type of information for the stated purpose.

  • Match and save data: Processed locally — purpose saving match progress, restoring games, and providing save slots; method local read/write; scope match state and save content related to the app.
  • Statistics and achievement data: Processed locally — purpose displaying win rates, challenge progress, and achievement records; method local storage; scope non-identifying data related to app statistics.
  • App settings and preferences: Processed locally — purpose remembering your theme, sound, and other UI preferences; method local storage; scope app configuration items.
  • Purchase and subscription status: Returned by app stores — purpose unlocking Pro benefits; method app-store verification; scope order status and product IDs.
  • Device and system information (such as device model, OS version, and app version): purpose feature compatibility, UI adaptation, and local diagnostics; method system API access or local logging; scope parameters necessary for functionality.
  • Network connectivity: Used when needed for app-store purchase verification, restore purchases, or subscription status checks — purpose entitlement verification; method app-store communication; scope network requests necessary for purchase verification.
  • Clipboard: We write content to the system clipboard only when you actively trigger a copy action (for example, copying a challenge seed or match summary). We do not read or upload clipboard content in the background.
  • Calendar, SMS, contacts, phone number, call logs, location information: Not collected.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA, OAID, etc.): The Service currently does not integrate advertising SDKs and does not collect these identifiers for advertising purposes.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The iOS / iPadOS / macOS / visionOS builds offer RetroSpider Pro monthly subscriptions, annual subscriptions, or a lifetime purchase through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Payment flows are handled by Apple; we do not collect or store your full payment card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials. We receive only purchase confirmation, subscription status, and product identifiers to unlock Pro benefits (such as premium themes, table backgrounds, and card backs) on your device. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Android builds may offer Pro benefits through Google Play Billing or billing provided by your distribution channel. Payment flows are handled by the relevant store; we do not collect or store your full payment card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials.

HarmonyOS builds may offer Pro benefits through Huawei AppGallery IAP or billing provided by your distribution channel. Payment flows are handled by the relevant store; we do not collect or store your full payment card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials.

Core gameplay is free for all users. Pro benefits mainly unlock cosmetic content, as shown in the app.

After you uninstall the app or clear data, corresponding local information is deleted, but order records handled by app stores remain subject to those platforms' policies.
