LocalAI will update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When you agree to the LocalAI service use agreement, you are deemed to have agreed to the entire content of this privacy policy. This Privacy Policy is an integral part of the LocalAI Service Use Agreement.

2. You understand and agree that the following information does not apply to this Privacy Policy:

  • (a) The keyword information you enter when using the search service provided by the LocalAI platform;
  • (b) The relevant information data collected by LocalAI and posted by you in LocalAI, including but not limited to participation activities, transaction information and evaluation details;
  • (c) Violation of the law or LocalAI rules and actions LocalAI has taken against you.

  • (c) For the purpose of serving users, LocalAI may use your personal information to provide you with information of interest to you, including but not limited to sending you product and service information, or sharing information with LocalAI partners so that they can send you relevant information on its products and services (the latter requires your prior consent).

4. Information disclosure

In the following cases, LocalAI will disclose your personal information in whole or in part according to your personal wishes or the provisions of the law:

  • (a) with your prior consent, to third parties;
  • (b) In order to provide the products and services you have requested, it is necessary to share your personal information with third parties;
  • (c) Disclosure to third parties or administrative or judicial institutions in accordance with the relevant provisions of the law, or the requirements of administrative or judicial institutions;
  • (d) If you violate relevant Chinese laws, regulations or LocalAI service agreement or related rules, you need to disclose it to a third party;

The information and data about you collected by LocalAI will be stored on the servers of LocalAI and/or its affiliates, and these information and data may be transmitted to and accessed abroad in your country, region or outside the place where LocalAI collects the information and data, storage and display.

LocalAI uses cookies to provide you with more thoughtful and personalized services, including promotional services.
  • (b) You have the right to choose to accept or reject cookies. You can refuse to accept cookies by modifying your browser settings. However, if you choose to refuse cookies, you may not be able to log in or use LocalAI web services or features that rely on cookies.
  • (c) This policy applies to the information obtained through the cookies set by LocalAI.

7. Information security

  • (a) LocalAI accounts have security protection functions, please keep your user name and password information properly. LocalAI will ensure that your information will not be lost, abused and altered by encrypting user passwords and other security measures. Please properly protect your personal information and only provide it to others when necessary. If you find that your personal information is leaked, especially the LocalAI username and password, please contact LocalAI customer service immediately so that LocalAI can take corresponding measures.

If you find that your personal information is leaked, especially the LocalAI username and password, please contact LocalAI customer service immediately or write an email to weigantechnology@outlook.com so that LocalAI can take corresponding measures.

When you make in-app purchases or subscribe to our services, all transactions are processed through Apple's App Store payment system. We do not collect or store your payment details, such as credit card numbers. We only receive purchase confirmation and subscription status from Apple to unlock the premium features for your account.
