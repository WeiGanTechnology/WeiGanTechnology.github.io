Privacy Statement for iEmulator

Last updated:2026.8.3

iEmulator is an application provided by Weigan Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "we" or "us") for retro game emulation. This Privacy Statement applies to iEmulator on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, and Android.

We attach great importance to the protection of your personal information and privacy. We will provide appropriate security measures for your personal information in accordance with legal requirements and industry-standard security practices.

This Privacy Statement works together with our User Agreement for iEmulator.

On the iOS / iPadOS build, advertising and usage-quota features apply to non-VIP users. When your detected region is mainland China, ads are served through Pangle GroMore mediation (including Pangle and Tencent Ads); in other regions, Google AdMob is used. VIP purchases are offered through Apple In-App Purchase. The macOS build does not display ads or apply daily launch quotas, but launching games requires an active VIP subscription.

On Android, Premium / VIP subscriptions and lifetime purchases are offered through Google Play Billing when distributed via Google Play or similar channels. When distributed via domestic app stores in mainland China (such as OPPO), non-Premium / non-VIP users may see ads served through Pangle GroMore mediation (see Section 2.3); Premium / VIP purchases in those channels are handled by the respective store billing systems. Game ROMs, save data, and related information are handled primarily on your device; iCloud sync is not available on Android. We obtain your consent before initializing any ad SDK.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Information

We will only use your personal information where we have a lawful basis to do so. Under applicable law, we may process your information based on lawful grounds such as your consent, where necessary to perform or enter into a contract with you, or where necessary to comply with legal obligations.

1.1 Core app features. To provide emulation, save management, netplay, controller support, and related features, we invoke Bluetooth, microphone, local network, and iCloud capabilities through system APIs only when you use the related features (on Android, storage, network, and Wi-Fi related capabilities as applicable). On iOS / iPadOS, we may request photo-library add permission when you save in-game screenshots; on Android, screenshots are saved primarily to app storage and may be saved to your gallery if you grant permission. Game ROM files, save data, screenshots, and other content you import are processed on your device or through services you actively choose. During netplay you initiate, game data may be transferred directly between your device and other players on your local network. Optional iCloud save sync (iOS / iPadOS / macOS only) and RetroAchievements (VIP / Premium subscribers only) are offered according to each platform's capabilities. We do not use this data to build a profile that identifies you.

1.2 Local preferences. We store certain settings on your device (such as theme, usage quota counters, VIP status flags, and emulator preferences) to operate the app. This information remains on your device unless otherwise described in this Statement.

1.3 Region detection for ads (iOS / iPadOS; may also apply on Android when ads are enabled and region detection is needed). To choose the appropriate advertising provider, the app may request your country or region code from a third-party IP geolocation service (ipinfo.io). This request may expose your IP address to that service. If the request fails or no stored value exists, we may fall back to your device language (for example, Simplified Chinese maps to mainland China). The resulting region code is stored locally on your device.

1.4 Cover art lookup. To display game cover art, the app may send a cleaned game title derived from your ROM filename to third-party services such as IGDB (via Twitch OAuth) and the Apple iTunes Search API. We do not send your ROM file contents. Retrieved cover images are cached locally on your device.

1.5 Optional third-party sign-in. If you are a VIP / Premium subscriber and choose to use RetroAchievements, your username and authentication token are stored in secure device storage (Keychain on iOS / macOS; Android Keystore or equivalent on Android) and transmitted to RetroAchievements servers to sign you in and sync achievements. We do not operate RetroAchievements; its processing is governed by RetroAchievements policies.

1.6 What we do not do. We do not require you to create an in-app account. On iOS / iPadOS, non-VIP users may use core emulation features subject to usage limits. On macOS, launching games requires an active VIP subscription. On Android, core emulation does not require an account; Premium / VIP entitlements are unlocked through Google Play Billing, domestic app-store billing, or equivalent channels where applicable. We do not provide, include, or distribute copyrighted game ROM files or other third-party content through the app.

1.7 Personal information inventory. To meet applicable privacy laws and app-store compliance requirements, we disclose below the categories of personal information that iEmulator may involve. The purpose, method, and scope described for each category apply only to the versions or scenarios noted. Categories marked as "not collected" mean that our app does not actively collect, read, or upload that type of information for the stated purpose.

  • Device identifiers (IDFV; IDFA where authorized; OAID and Android ID on Android): On iOS / iPadOS, advertising SDKs may automatically collect device identifiers — purpose ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; method automatic SDK collection; scope parameters necessary for advertising services. IDFA is used only where you authorize App Tracking Transparency (ATT). The macOS build does not integrate advertising SDKs for this purpose. On Android builds distributed in mainland China with ads enabled, related SDKs (such as the Pangle Advertising SDK, Tencent Ads Android SDK, and Douyin SDK (Android)) may collect identifiers such as OAID and Android ID — purpose ad delivery, performance measurement, anti-fraud, and SDK stability; method automatic SDK collection; scope parameters necessary for advertising services, subject to your consent and Section 2.3 below.
  • IP address: On iOS / iPadOS, Google AdMob or Pangle / Tencent Ads SDKs may automatically collect IP addresses when serving ads. On Android builds distributed in mainland China with ads enabled, the Pangle Advertising SDK, Tencent Ads Android SDK, Douyin SDK (Android), and related SDKs may automatically collect IP addresses — purpose ad loading, anti-fraud, and SDK stability; method automatic SDK collection; scope network addresses related to ad services. The app may also send your IP address to ipinfo.io when determining your advertising region — purpose region detection for ad provider selection; method network request initiated by the app; scope country or region code returned by the service.
  • Sensor list and specific sensor types (such as accelerometer and gyroscope): On iOS / iPadOS, Google AdMob may perform such collection when serving ads. We configure the Pangle SDK not to use location for advertising.
  • Location information: Not collected by us for advertising. We do not request location permission for ad targeting.
  • Clipboard: We write content to the system clipboard only when you actively trigger a copy action (for example, copying our support email address). We do not read or upload clipboard content in the background.
  • Game title for cover lookup: When fetching cover art, we may send a cleaned game title derived from your ROM filename to IGDB and the Apple iTunes Search API — purpose retrieving publicly available cover images; method network request initiated by the app; scope game title text only, not ROM file contents.
  • Images and photo library: On iOS / iPadOS, when you take a screenshot in-game, the app may request photo-library add permission to save the image to your album; if permission is denied, the screenshot is saved to app storage instead. On Android, screenshots are saved to app storage by default and may be saved to your gallery if you grant permission. We do not read your entire photo library in the background.
  • Microphone: Used only when a game or core you are running requires microphone input (for example, some Nintendo DS titles). Audio is processed locally for emulation and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Video and audio in ads: Rewarded and other ad formats may load and play media through third-party ad SDKs on iOS / iPadOS (and on Android when ads are enabled) when you watch ads. We do not collect or upload your personal video or audio files through ads.
  • Game ROM files and save data: Processed locally on your device. On iOS / iPadOS / macOS, save data may be stored in your iCloud container when you enable sync. The Android build currently stores save data on your device only. We do not upload your ROM library to our servers.
  • Calendar, SMS, contacts, phone number, call logs: Not collected.
