Privacy Statement for iEmulator

Last updated:2026.7.29

iEmulator is an application provided by Weigan Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "we" or "us") for retro game emulation on Apple platforms. This Privacy Statement applies to iEmulator on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS.

We attach great importance to the protection of your personal information and privacy. We will provide appropriate security measures for your personal information in accordance with legal requirements and industry-standard security practices.

This Privacy Statement works together with our User Agreement for iEmulator.

On the iOS / iPadOS build, advertising and usage-quota features apply to non-VIP users. When your detected region is mainland China, ads are served through Pangle GroMore mediation (including Pangle and Tencent Ads); in other regions, Google AdMob is used. VIP purchases are offered through Apple In-App Purchase. The macOS build does not display ads or apply daily launch quotas, but launching games requires an active VIP subscription.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Information

We will only use your personal information where we have a lawful basis to do so. Under applicable law, we may process your information based on lawful grounds such as your consent, where necessary to perform or enter into a contract with you, or where necessary to comply with legal obligations.

1.1 Core app features. To provide emulation, save management, netplay, controller support, and related features, we invoke Bluetooth, microphone, local network, and iCloud capabilities through system APIs only when you use the related features. On iOS / iPadOS, we may request photo-library add permission when you save in-game screenshots. Game ROM files, save data, screenshots, and other content you import are processed on your device or through services you actively choose. During netplay you initiate, game data may be transferred directly between your device and other players on your local network. Optional iCloud save sync and RetroAchievements are available only to VIP subscribers. We do not use this data to build a profile that identifies you.

1.2 Local preferences. We store certain settings on your device (such as theme, usage quota counters, VIP status flags, and emulator preferences) to operate the app. This information remains on your device unless otherwise described in this Statement.

1.3 Region detection for ads (iOS / iPadOS only). To choose the appropriate advertising provider, the app may request your country or region code from a third-party IP geolocation service (ipinfo.io). This request may expose your IP address to that service. If the request fails or no stored value exists, we may fall back to your device language (for example, Simplified Chinese maps to mainland China). The resulting region code is stored locally on your device.

1.4 Cover art lookup. To display game cover art, the app may send a cleaned game title derived from your ROM filename to third-party services such as IGDB (via Twitch OAuth) and the Apple iTunes Search API. We do not send your ROM file contents. Retrieved cover images are cached locally on your device.

1.5 Optional third-party sign-in. If you are a VIP subscriber and choose to use RetroAchievements, your username and authentication token are stored locally in the device keychain and transmitted to RetroAchievements servers to sign you in and sync achievements. We do not operate RetroAchievements; its processing is governed by RetroAchievements policies.

1.6 What we do not do. We do not require you to create an in-app account. On iOS / iPadOS, non-VIP users may use core emulation features subject to usage limits. On macOS, launching games requires an active VIP subscription. We do not provide, include, or distribute copyrighted game ROM files or other third-party content through the app.

1.7 Personal information inventory. To meet applicable privacy laws and app-store compliance requirements, we disclose below the categories of personal information that iEmulator may involve. The purpose, method, and scope described for each category apply only to the versions or scenarios noted. Categories marked as "not collected" mean that our app does not actively collect, read, or upload that type of information for the stated purpose.

  • Device identifiers (IDFV; IDFA where authorized): On iOS / iPadOS, advertising SDKs may automatically collect device identifiers — purpose ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; method automatic SDK collection; scope parameters necessary for advertising services. IDFA is used only where you authorize App Tracking Transparency (ATT). The macOS build does not integrate advertising SDKs for this purpose.
  • IP address: On iOS / iPadOS, Google AdMob or Pangle / Tencent Ads SDKs may automatically collect IP addresses when serving ads — purpose ad loading, anti-fraud, and SDK stability; method automatic SDK collection; scope network addresses related to ad services. The app may also send your IP address to ipinfo.io when determining your advertising region — purpose region detection for ad provider selection; method network request initiated by the app; scope country or region code returned by the service.
  • Sensor list and specific sensor types (such as accelerometer and gyroscope): On iOS / iPadOS, Google AdMob may perform such collection when serving ads. We configure the Pangle SDK not to use location for advertising.
  • Location information: Not collected by us for advertising. We do not request location permission for ad targeting.
  • Clipboard: We write content to the system clipboard only when you actively trigger a copy action (for example, copying our support email address). We do not read or upload clipboard content in the background.
  • Game title for cover lookup: When fetching cover art, we may send a cleaned game title derived from your ROM filename to IGDB and the Apple iTunes Search API — purpose retrieving publicly available cover images; method network request initiated by the app; scope game title text only, not ROM file contents.
  • Images and photo library: On iOS / iPadOS, when you take a screenshot in-game, the app may request photo-library add permission to save the image to your album; if permission is denied, the screenshot is saved to app storage instead. We do not read your entire photo library in the background.
  • Microphone: Used only when a game or core you are running requires microphone input (for example, some Nintendo DS titles). Audio is processed locally for emulation and is not uploaded to our servers.
  • Video and audio in ads: Rewarded and other ad formats may load and play media through third-party ad SDKs on iOS / iPadOS when you watch ads. We do not collect or upload your personal video or audio files through ads.
  • Game ROM files and save data: Processed locally on your device or in your iCloud container when you enable sync. We do not upload your ROM library to our servers.
  • Calendar, SMS, contacts, phone number, call logs: Not collected.

In this scenario, the app integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display ads (including app-open and rewarded formats), measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. AdMob is provided by Google LLC and may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version, and IDFV): Purpose — ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — parameters necessary for advertising services.
  • Advertising identifiers (advertising-related identifiers on iOS where permitted by the system): Purpose — ad measurement and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — device-assigned advertising-related identifiers. You can limit ad tracking or reset advertising identifiers in your device settings.
  • Network and diagnostic information (such as IP address, network type, and crash or diagnostic signals related to ad serving): Purpose — ad loading, fraud prevention, and SDK stability; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — data related to ad delivery and SDK operation.
  • Sensor list and specific sensor types (such as accelerometer and gyroscope): Purpose — ad anti-fraud, performance optimization, and device compatibility checks; Method — automatic collection by the SDK through system APIs; Scope — sensor types and list information directly related to advertising and anti-fraud.

On iOS / iPadOS in the AdMob scenario described above, the app does not currently present an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before showing AdMob ads. You can limit personalized ads or reset advertising identifiers in iOS system settings (for example, Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

VIP subscribers do not see ads for features covered by their active subscription.

2.2 Pangle GroMore mediation (iOS / iPadOS: detected region is mainland China)

In this scenario, the app does not use AdMob. It initializes third-party advertising SDKs through Pangle GroMore mediation, which may serve ads from the following ad networks we have integrated:

  • Pangle (Chuanshanjia) — Beijing Juliang Engine Network Technology Co., Ltd.
  • Tencent Ads (Youlianghui / GDT) — Shenzhen Tencent Computer Systems Co., Ltd.

GroMore selects and fills ad requests across these networks for the ad placements you trigger in the app. The SDKs automatically collect information such as:

  • Device information (such as device model and operating system version; IDFV on iOS, and IDFA on iOS where you authorize ATT): Purpose — ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — parameters necessary for advertising services.
  • Network state information (such as network type and connectivity): Purpose — ad loading and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — data related to network connectivity.
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic information (such as impressions, clicks, conversions, and SDK operational logs): Purpose — ad measurement, settlement, and troubleshooting; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — data directly related to ad services.
  • IP address: Purpose — ad loading, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection during network communication by the SDK; Scope — network addresses related to ad services.

SDK providers and privacy policies:

  • Pangle / GroMore: https://www.csjplatform.com/privacy/partner
  • Tencent Ads (GDT): https://e.qq.com/dev/help_detail.html?cid=2005&pid=5983

On iOS / iPadOS (mainland China region), we configure the Pangle SDK privacy provider to disable use of location for advertising. Before initializing the Pangle SDK, if the system has not yet recorded your choice, the app may present an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt to determine whether IDFA may be used. If you deny or restrict tracking, we configure the SDK to limit IDFA use. VIP subscribers do not see ads.

Third-party SDKs may transmit the above information to their servers for processing and storage. Please refer to each provider's privacy policy for details.

3. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The iOS / iPadOS and macOS builds offer VIP subscriptions and a lifetime purchase through Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Payment flows are handled by Apple; we do not collect or store your full payment card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials.

We receive only purchase confirmation, subscription status, and product identifiers to unlock VIP features (such as ad-free experience on iOS / iPadOS, unlimited game launches, theme and interface customization, iCloud save sync, and RetroAchievements access) on your device. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Apple's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's policies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

On iOS / iPadOS, non-VIP users may be subject to daily free launch limits and can earn additional launches by watching rewarded video ads. On macOS, launching games requires an active VIP subscription.

4. Device Permission Usage

This service may request the following permissions on iOS, iPadOS, and/or macOS. We ask for your consent through the system permission dialog before using sensitive capabilities. Permissions you deny will limit only the related features.

Bluetooth

Used to discover and connect to external game controllers for gameplay and controller configuration tools.

Photo library (add only, iOS / iPadOS)

Used when you save an in-game screenshot to your photo album. If you deny this permission, screenshots are saved to app storage instead.

Microphone

Used to support sound input in games or cores that require microphone access (such as some Nintendo DS titles). Audio is processed locally for emulation.

Local network

Used to discover and connect to other players for rollback netplay over your local network (Bonjour).

iCloud

Used when VIP subscribers enable save-data sync. Save files are stored in your iCloud container and managed according to Apple's iCloud policies.

App Tracking Transparency (iOS / iPadOS)

May be requested before initializing Pangle advertising SDKs in the mainland China ad scenario, to determine whether IDFA may be used.

Internet

Used for ads (iOS / iPadOS non-VIP users), App Store billing, optional RetroAchievements sign-in (VIP only), region detection for ads, automatic or manual cover-art lookup, and netplay.

You can manage permissions for this app at any time in your device Settings.
