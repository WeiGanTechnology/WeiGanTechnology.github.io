1. Description of the Service

1.1 TiYi is a multi-protocol VPN client utility for connecting, on your device, to proxy nodes, remote configuration URLs, or VPN servers that you import yourself. Features may include, without limitation:

  • Multi-protocol connectivity: Shadowsocks (SS), VMess, VLESS, Trojan, Hysteria / Hysteria2 (Hy2), TUIC, WireGuard (WG), OpenVPN, and IKEv2, as supported in your build;
  • Profile import: on iOS, iPadOS, and macOS, remote configuration URLs, share links, Clash / sing-box configurations, OpenVPN files, pasted text, local files, and URL schemes (such as ss, vmess, or vless); on iOS / iPadOS, QR-code scanning as well. On Apple TV, typing a link or configuration text with the remote, adding a node manually, entering IKEv2 details, or syncing from an iPhone or Mac that has iCloud turned on;
  • Node management: switching among nodes from a remote configuration and measuring latency;
  • Routing modes: rule, global, or direct;
  • System proxy and routing scope: injecting an HTTP proxy through the VPN tunnel. On iOS you can choose whether to cover all network interfaces (which may include cellular and local networks). Apple TV does not offer a system include-all-networks switch; when LAN bypass is on, on-device tunnel rules send private ranges direct;
  • Optional iCloud sync: after you turn it on, profiles sync in your own iCloud so you can use them across devices, including Apple TV;
  • Unlock: a subscription or one-time purchase processed by Apple unlocks unlimited connection time. Without a purchase, daily free connection time is limited;
  • IKEv2: connecting through the system VPN API with the server and account details you enter;
  • Connection status, traffic statistics, diagnostic logs, and optional update checks.

Available features depend on your app version, device, operating system, and distribution channel. Packet tunneling relies on the system Network Extension; IKEv2 relies on system VPN. Capabilities may differ by OS version. We may update, adjust, or discontinue features from time to time.

1.2 The Service is client software, not a VPN hosting service. We do not provide, host, or operate public proxy or VPN nodes, and we do not warrant the availability, speed, logging practices, or legality of any third-party node. Nodes, remote configurations, and servers are obtained and imported by you; your relationship with those third parties is governed by their own terms.

1.3 Without a purchase, core connectivity remains available, but only for a limited free time each day (currently 5 minutes, counting only time the tunnel is actually connected, as shown in the app). When that time runs out, the connection stops. You can unlock unlimited connection time with an auto-renewable subscription or a one-time purchase processed by Apple. On iOS / iPadOS, third-party ads may appear until you unlock, and watching a rewarded video can extend that day's free time. After unlock, rewarded videos are no longer used for that purpose. Apple TV and macOS do not integrate third-party advertising, and Apple TV cannot extend free time by watching a video. If paid items or ad formats change, we will update this Agreement and the Privacy Policy as required by law.

1.4 The Service does not require you to create an in-app account operated by us, and it does not provide a cloud account operated by us. Profiles are stored primarily in the main app. The compiled configuration needed for the current connection, some preferences, and logs may be shared between the app and the Network Extension through an App Group. On Apple TV, those shared files are in the App Group's Library/Caches directory. iCloud sync is off by default. After you turn on automatic sync or run a manual sync, profiles (which may include node keys and the IKEv2 password) are written to your own iCloud and stored by Apple. They are not uploaded to our servers.

1.5 After VPN is enabled, device network traffic may be forwarded through the node you select and may affect the network path of some or all applications. The Service does not guarantee that connections will always succeed, that speed will meet a particular need, that traffic cannot be observed by third parties, or that you can circumvent any network restriction, censorship, or legal obligation. Because of system limits, Apple TV cannot cover every network interface through a system switch the way iOS can.

1.6 What is different on Apple TV. The Apple TV app connects the television to nodes you import yourself or receive through iCloud sync. It does not offer camera scanning, clipboard access, system file import, encrypted-backup import or export, notification alerts, or a system review prompt. Widgets and the share extension are not available on Apple TV. Type a configuration with the remote, or import it on an iPhone or Mac and then sync it to the television.
