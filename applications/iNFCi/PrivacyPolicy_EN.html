Privacy Statement for iNFCi

Last updated:2026.7.13

iNFCi is an application provided by Weigan Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as "we" or "us") for NFC read/write and related device connectivity features. This Privacy Statement explains how we process information when you use the Google Play version of iNFCi.

We attach great importance to the protection of your personal information and privacy. We will provide appropriate security measures for your personal information in accordance with legal requirements and industry-standard security practices.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Information

We will only use your personal information where we have a lawful basis to do so. Under applicable law, we may process your information based on lawful grounds such as your consent, where necessary to perform or enter into a contract with you, or where necessary to comply with legal obligations.

1.1 Core app features. To provide NFC read/write, Bluetooth device connection, firmware updates, QR code scanning, and LAN-based device updates, we invoke NFC, Bluetooth, camera, and network capabilities through system APIs only when you use the related features. NFC tag data, device communication payloads, and files you choose to read or write are processed on your device or between your device and the peripherals you connect. We do not use this data to build a profile that identifies you.

1.2 Local preferences. We store certain settings on your device (such as theme, usage quota counters, and VIP status flags) to operate the app. This information remains on your device unless otherwise described in this Statement.

1.3 What we do not do. We do not require you to create an account to use iNFCi. We do not provide, include, or distribute copyrighted game files or other third-party content through the app.

2. Third-Party SDKs and Advertising (Google Play version)

The Google Play version of iNFCi integrates Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display ads (including app-open and rewarded ads), measure ad performance, and prevent fraud. AdMob is provided by Google LLC and may automatically collect information such as:

  • Device information (such as device model, operating system version, and Android ID): Purpose — ad delivery, performance measurement, and anti-fraud; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — parameters necessary for advertising services.
  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID): Purpose — ad personalization and measurement where permitted; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — the advertising identifier assigned by your device. You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings.
  • Network and diagnostic information (such as IP address, network type, and crash/diagnostic signals related to ad serving): Purpose — ad loading, fraud prevention, and SDK stability; Method — automatic collection by the SDK; Scope — data related to ad delivery and SDK operation.

Google may process this information on servers located in countries/regions where Google operates. For details, see Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Consent and ad privacy controls. Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland), we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before serving personalized ads. Depending on your choices, ads may be limited or non-personalized. You can review or change your ad privacy choices at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy settings (when available for your region). Ads are requested only when the consent state allows ad requests.

VIP subscribers do not see ads for features covered by their subscription. Non-VIP users may see ads as part of the free usage model.

We receive only purchase confirmation, subscription status, and product identifiers from Google Play to unlock VIP features (such as ad-free experience, theme access, and expanded usage) on your device. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Google Play account settings.

Google's handling of payment data is governed by Google's policies: Google Payments Terms of Service

4. Device Permission Usage

This service may request the following permissions. We ask for your consent through the system permission dialog before using sensitive capabilities. Permissions you deny will limit only the related features.

NFC

Used to read from and write to NFC tags and cards when you use NFC features.

Bluetooth (scan and connect)

Used to discover and connect to external Bluetooth devices (such as Pixl.js) for data transfer and firmware updates. On Android 12 and above, Bluetooth scan is declared as not used for location purposes.

Location (Android 11 and below only)

On older Android versions, the operating system requires location permission before Bluetooth device scanning. This permission is not requested on Android 12 and above for Bluetooth scanning in this app. If you deny location permission on a supported older version, Bluetooth scanning will not work, but other app features remain available.

Camera

Used for QR code scanning when you choose that feature. Camera images are processed locally for decoding and are not uploaded to our servers.

Internet and network state

Used for ads, Google Play Billing, firmware or content downloads you initiate, and LAN-based device updates.

Nearby Wi-Fi devices

Used for LAN discovery and local network communication when you use LAN OTA or similar on-device networking features.

Notifications

Used to show optional status notifications related to background device operations (such as Bluetooth connections or updates), when you grant notification permission.

Foreground service (connected device)

Used to keep Bluetooth or device-connection tasks running reliably while you are actively using related features.

You can manage permissions for this app at any time in your device Settings.
